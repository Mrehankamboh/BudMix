Overview

  1. IThe problem

    Most buds hold one connection.

    Earbuds without multipoint talk to one device at a time. While they play the phone, the laptop can't reach them at all.

  2. IIThe old way

    Settings, disconnect, settings, connect.

    Every switch means opening Bluetooth, disconnecting from one device and connecting on the other. Then doing it all again to switch back.

  3. IIIWith BudMix

    Your buds never leave the laptop.

    The phone sends its sound to the laptop over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, and BudMix mixes it with the laptop's own sound. Nothing switches.

How it connects

Two ways in. One way out.

The phone reaches the laptop over Wi-Fi when it casts, and over Bluetooth the rest of the time. The laptop sends the mix to your buds.

  • Phone over Wi-Fi

    The phone casts with its built-in Cast button. The sound travels over a direct Wi-Fi link, even with no internet.

  • Phone over Bluetooth

    The laptop acts as a Bluetooth speaker for a paired phone. BudMix opens the link itself when the phone isn't casting.

  • Laptop to buds

    Your buds stay connected to the laptop in stereo. BudMix never opens their microphone.

Phone over Wi-Fi

Uses Miracast, the screen-casting standard built into most Android phones. BudMix is the receiver, so the phone sends its sound and screen to the laptop.

Steps

  1. On the phone, swipe down twice and tap Cast (Samsung: Smart View).
  2. Tap BudMix in the list.
  3. The first time, click Allow always, then Allow sound. Windows asks once.

Phone over Bluetooth

Windows can act as a Bluetooth speaker for a phone. BudMix uses that, and connects the phone by itself whenever it isn't casting.

Steps

  1. Pair the phone once: Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Add device.
  2. Leave Phone connection on Auto in BudMix.
  3. Play something on the phone. Nothing else to tap.

Your buds

The buds connect to the laptop only. BudMix makes them the laptop's output and keeps them there.

Steps

  1. Disconnect the buds from the phone.
  2. Take them out of the case and click Buds in the BudMix dock.
  3. Click Test beep to check.

Features

Quiet in the tray. Ready when you are.

  • Reconnects by itself

    Buds, phone and receiver come back after a drop, a closed case or sleep.

  • A volume for each source

    Buds, laptop, phone over Wi-Fi and phone over Bluetooth, each with a slider and mute.

  • One-click setup

    Checks Bluetooth, pairing, the firewall and Wi-Fi support, and fixes what it can.

  • Any network

    Home, office or a network it has never seen. Casting even works with no internet.

  • Your phones only

    A new phone plays only after you allow it on the laptop. Forget a phone anytime.

  • Clear messages

    "Phone is casting", "Allow the phone's sound?", "Buds switched to call mode".

  • Hotkey

    Ctrl+Alt+B connects or releases the buds from any app.

  • Starts with Windows

    Waits in the tray with the receiver ready, and connects the buds when they're on.

  • Private by design

    No account, no telemetry. Nothing is sent to the internet.

Honest notes

What it can't do

  • A little delayFine for music and laptop videos. A video on the phone's own screen will be slightly out of lip-sync.
  • Two phones on BluetoothBoth can connect, but when both play at once the sound can break up.
  • Some apps block castingNetflix, for example. Use Bluetooth for those.
  • Calls lower the qualityWhen an app uses the buds' microphone, Bluetooth drops to call quality. BudMix tells you.
  • Not code-signed yetWindows warns the first time; Smart App Control blocks unsigned apps.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Ask on GitHub and we'll help.

Ask a question
Do I install anything on my phone?

No. BudMix runs on the Windows laptop only. The phone uses its built-in Cast button, or Bluetooth after pairing once.

Does it work on any Wi-Fi?

Yes: home, office or a network the laptop has never seen, even with no internet. The first time a phone casts, BudMix asks to allow its sound and Windows confirms once.

Is it really free?

Yes. BudMix is free and open source under the MIT license. No account, no ads, no paid version.

Does it send anything over the internet?

No. Everything stays between your laptop, your phone and your buds. There is no telemetry.

Does my phone's speaker go quiet while casting?

Usually yes: Android sends media sound to the cast. Ringtones and notifications may still play on the phone.

Can I use it with an iPhone?

Over Bluetooth only, since iPhones don't support Miracast. We haven't tested an iPhone yet.

My buds support multipoint. Do I need this?

Probably not. Multipoint buds already stay connected to two devices. BudMix is for the many that don't.
